Fort Falkland is one of Banagher's important historic sites. A dedicated group of volunteers gave up their Saturday mornings for months, carefully removing decades of overgrown vegetation that had completely engulfed the site.

Since being cleared, Fort Falkland has hosted several community events and gatherings, proving just how much potential this unique location holds. It is a flexible, atmospheric space with endless possibilities — cultural events, heritage projects, outdoor performances, community workshops, and more.

The site is currently owned by Offaly County Council, and we are actively promoting Fort Falkland as a key element of the wider Marina redevelopment plan.
