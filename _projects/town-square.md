The aim is to transform Banagher's Town Square into a more vibrant and welcoming public space. Plans include improved seating, additional greenery, and thoughtful design elements that will make the area more welcoming and instantly recognisable as the true centre of the town.

A grant application has already been submitted to secure funding for this work.
